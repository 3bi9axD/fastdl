Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

This policy explains what information TyBot may process when you use the bot on Discord.

1. Information TyBot may process

TyBot may process Discord user IDs, usernames, server IDs, channel IDs, role IDs, message IDs, server configuration, moderation warnings, TyCoin balances and transactions, XP and levels, giveaway participation, marketplace purchases, Premium Room and TempVoice configuration, and saved language preferences.

2. Message content

TyBot may inspect message content when needed for commands, anti-spam, anti-link, bad-word filtering, XP, or other enabled features. TyBot does not sell message content or use it for advertising.

3. How data is used

Data is used to operate TyBot features, remember settings, prevent abuse, process TyCoin actions, run moderation systems and maintain reliability.

4. Data sharing

TyBot does not sell personal information. Data may be processed by hosting or infrastructure providers required to operate the service, or disclosed when required by law.

5. Retention and deletion

Stored data may be retained while needed for the related feature. Users or server owners may request deletion of applicable TyBot data through the support section below.

6. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect stored data, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Age requirements

TyBot is intended for users permitted to use Discord under Discord's Terms of Service and minimum-age requirements.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated when TyBot features or legal requirements change. The newest version will remain on this page.

9. Contact

For privacy or data requests, use the Support section on this website.
